# Data Analytics Portfolio

## About me

I am a CFO and business leader with extensive experience in finance, risk management, construction, and agribusiness.  
In 2022, I had the opportunity to work closely with my first IT product — and that moment fundamentally reshaped my professional trajectory.

What became clear to me very quickly:  
**data analytics is the natural intersection of finance, experimentation, uncertainty, and decision-making under risk.**

I bring into analytics what many purely technical profiles lack:

- economic thinking,
- understanding of trade-offs,
- experience working with incomplete information,
- and responsibility for real financial outcomes.

I do not see analytics as reporting or tooling.  
I see it as **a decision-support system for business operating in conditions of constant change**.

## My perspective on data & IT

I have spent years organizing chaos:
- in financial models,
- in business processes,
- in teams operating under pressure and uncertainty.

**Data analytics is a modern form of the same skill** — transforming fragmented signals into simple, explainable models that support decisions.

I have seen how expensive intuition-based decisions can be.  
Working with data means replacing assumptions with facts — and that directly impacts profitability, sustainability, and growth.

IT, for me, is not a career reset.

## What you will find in this repository

This repository contains **end-to-end analytics projects**, designed and executed with a business mindset:

- clearly defined business problems,
- transparent metrics and assumptions,
- reproducible analysis,
- insights translated into actionable recommendations.

Each project follows a consistent structure:

- business context and goals,
- data sources and limitations,
- analytical approach,
- results and anomalies,
- recommendations and expected impact.

This is not a collection of notebooks.  
This is a portfolio of **decision-oriented analytics work**.
